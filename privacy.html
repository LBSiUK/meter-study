Privacy policy

Last updated 30 October 2026.

Meter Study is a private Mac app used by two University of Sussex students. This page says what it does with Google data.

What it reads from Google

When you link Google Calendar, Meter Study reads your email address, your list of calendars and the events in them. It uses them to find when you are busy, and to compare the copies of your Sussex events with your timetable.

What it changes in Google

It creates one calendar called Meter Study, and adds a study session there when you accept one. If you turn on the fixing option, it also corrects the time or room of a Sussex event when your timetable shows a change. With that option off, it changes nothing else in your calendars.

Where the data goes

Your Google sign-in token is encrypted and kept on a private server run by the app's two users. Calendar details are kept there so the app can plan your day. Nothing is sold, shared with anyone else or used for advertising, and Google data is not used to train AI models.

Meter Study's use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How to remove it

Remove Google in the app's Settings, under Accounts. You can also take away access at any time in your Google account permissions.
